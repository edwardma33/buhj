# Buhj product context

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Buhj serves its owner and a small circle of invitees who manage their own finances.

## Product Purpose

Buhj is a free, invite-only budgeting app. It helps people understand monthly cash flow, see what drove spending, check observed pay against expectations, and explore what their savings could look like.

## Operating Context

- People add financial accounts, upload USD statement CSVs, review the preview and possible duplicates, and confirm the imports they want to use.
- The main return visit starts with the month’s spending. People check the account and date range, read the totals, then drill into transactions and correct the category or transaction type when needed.
- Income onboarding, completed-month pay review, Spending Insights, and Savings Projections support follow-up questions. Insights and Projections can be saved and reopened.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Monthly reports use confirmed transactions and their posted dates. Refunds count as inflows and reduce category spend. Internal transfers are excluded from cash flow and remain reviewable.
- Account and transaction information is private to the signed-in user. Signup requires an invitation.
- Use synthetic financial data and disposable environments for design previews and verification.

## Product Principles

- Make it easy to return to monthly spending and reach the transactions behind a number.
- Keep the scope of a financial number visible, including the month and selected accounts.
- Preserve the distinction between confirmed spending evidence, reported pay, and projections.
